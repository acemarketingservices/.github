## What Ace does

| Service | What the creator gets |
|---|---|
| **Growth operations** | A coordinated team and a repeatable plan instead of disconnected vendors. |
| **Content production** | Creative direction, editing, and AI assisted production with human review. |
| **Audience development** | Channel specific execution and systematic testing across every approved account. |
| **Monetization support** | Pricing, offers, retention, and fan revenue operations. |
| **Account care** | Platform compliance, account health tracking, and issue response. |
| **Operational visibility** | A portal that shows performance, earnings, invoices, tasks, and the team behind the work. |
